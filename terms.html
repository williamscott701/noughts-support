The first time you open Noughts, you can take the 10-day Trial, a free in-app item from the App Store. 3.Inkwell subscriptions

Inkwell monthly and Inkwell yearly are auto-renewing subscriptions that keep everything open. The price and length of each are shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. A subscription renews automatically for the same length and price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period, and the renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that end. You can manage or cancel it in your device's Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; access continues until the end of the period already paid for. Neither subscription includes a free trial period.
