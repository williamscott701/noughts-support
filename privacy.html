Noughts & Crosses

Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

1.No data is collected

Noughts does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it contains no third-party code that could do any of these.

2.What stays on your device

Your settings, your saved match and your records (games played, won, lost and drawn) are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are removed when you delete the app. They are not sent anywhere.

3.No purchases, no network

The app has nothing to buy and makes no network requests.

4.Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

5.Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: williamscott701@gmail.com.
