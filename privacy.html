Noughts & Crosses

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

1.No data is collected

Noughts does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any information about you. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and it contains no third-party code that could do any of these.

2.What stays on your device

Your settings, your saved match, your records (games played, won, lost and drawn) and your Workbook progress (levels, stars and daily streak) are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are removed when you delete the app. They are not sent anywhere.

3.Purchases

The 10-day trial and the Inkwell plans are bought through Apple's App Store, which handles payment. The developer receives no personal or payment information from it. Apart from the App Store, the app makes no network requests.

4.Children

Because nothing is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children.

5.Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: williamscott701@gmail.com.
