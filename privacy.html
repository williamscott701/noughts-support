Noughts & Crosses

Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 November 2026

1.In short

Noughts has no account, no ads and no tracking. It sends anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics for Firebase, which are not linked to you, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's Settings. Your games and progress stay on your device.

2.Anonymous usage statistics

To learn how the book is used and make it better, the app records events such as "trial started", "level completed", "daily puzzle solved", "plan page viewed" or "a setting changed", with a few details like the level number, stars, time taken, or which plan was chosen. They are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, Google's service for app statistics, along with the general information that service records for every app, such as the device model, the iOS version, the app version and the country.

They are not linked to you. The app never sends a name, an email address, a user ID, the advertising identifier (the part of Firebase that reads it is not included in the app) or the vendor identifier, and it turns off ad personalization and ad storage. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics storage on the device is also turned off. The statistics are not used for advertising and are not sold.

To stop them, open Settings in the app and untick "Share anonymous usage figures". Nothing more is sent from that moment on, including on later launches. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

3.What stays on your device

Your settings, your saved match, your records (games played, won, lost and drawn) and your Workbook progress (levels, stars and daily streak) are kept only on your device, in the app's own storage. They are removed when you delete the app.

4.Purchases

The 15-day trial and the Inkwell plans are bought through Apple's App Store, which handles payment. The developer receives no personal or payment information from it. To count purchases, the usage statistics include that a purchase was made, which plan, and its price and currency.

5.Children

The app asks for no personal information from anyone, children included, and the usage statistics are not linked to any person.
