Name

Noughts & Crosses

Subject
Three games of X and O

Noughts is an exercise book of noughts and crosses (tic tac toe) for iPhone and iPad. The Workbook has 120 levels of puzzles in twelve chapters, each chapter ending with a test against a classmate; today's puzzle is set at midnight; and there are three games, listed under Games on the contents page: Classic against Easy, Medium or Unbeatable, Ultimate with its nine boards, and Four in a row on a 4 × 4 grid, against the computer or a friend.

1.Every page open for 15 days, then Inkwell

For the first 15 days everything is open, at no cost and with no ads. Starting the 15-day trial costs nothing and does not turn into a subscription. After 15 days the whole game locks until you choose Inkwell: a monthly or yearly subscription, or Inkwell for life, a single payment that is yours for good. Your levels, stars, streak and records are kept on your device. There are no ads, ever, and no account. The prices are shown in the app before the trial starts. See the Terms of Use for the details.

2.Questions

  • How do I cancel a subscription? In the iPhone or iPad Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Inkwell. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next renewal.
  • I bought Inkwell on another device. Tap Restore Purchases, on the plan page or in the app's Settings, while signed in with the same Apple Account. Inkwell for life is shared with your family through Family Sharing.
  • Will reinstalling give me another 15 days? No. The 15 days run from the day the trial was first taken on your Apple Account.
  • What happens to my progress after the trial? It is kept: level, stars, streak, records and your saved match all come back when you choose a plan.
  • Is the daily puzzle the same for everyone? Yes, for everyone on the same date. A new one is set at midnight, your time.
  • Where is my saved match? Every move is saved on the device. It appears on the contents page as a note headed “Carry on”.
  • Can I beat Unbeatable? No: it has worked out the 3 × 3 game to the end. A draw is the best result there is.
  • Can I have a reminder for the daily puzzle? Yes. After your first daily puzzle the app offers one, at a time you choose; you can also switch it on in Settings, under Daily reminder. It comes at most once a day, only if the puzzle is still to do, and notification permission is asked only when you say yes.
  • What are "usage figures"? Anonymous statistics, sent through Google Analytics for Firebase, about how the book is used (for example, which levels are finished). They are not linked to you. Untick "Share anonymous usage figures" in Settings to stop them. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Keyboard? On iPad, the arrow keys move the pencil, Return writes, ⌘Z undoes and ⌘N starts the next game.
