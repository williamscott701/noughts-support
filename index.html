Name

Noughts & Crosses

Subject
Three games of X and O

Noughts is an exercise book of three games for iPhone and iPad: Classic noughts and crosses against Easy, Medium or Unbeatable, Ultimate with its nine boards, and Four in a row on a 4 × 4 grid. Play the computer or pass the phone to a friend.

1.Free, all of it

Every game is open from the first launch and stays that way. There are no ads, no purchases, no subscription and no account.

2.Questions

  • Where is my saved match? Every move is saved on the device. It appears on the contents page as a note headed “Carry on”.
  • Can I take a move back? Yes, Undo takes back your last move, and against the computer its reply too, until the game is decided.
  • Can I beat Unbeatable? No: it has worked out the 3 × 3 game to the end. A draw is the best result there is.
  • Keyboard? On iPad, the arrow keys move the pencil, Return writes, ⌘Z undoes and ⌘N starts the next game.
